# Product journeys

## Record a feed

Choose breast or bottle feeding, record the relevant timing and amount, and save the result to the shared history. Left and right breast controls retain their physical left/right arrangement. Manual entries support activities recorded afterward.

## Understand a day

Care cards provide activity-specific recent details. History brings records together using calendar views and filters. A rolling sleep summary helps caregivers review the preceding 24 hours.

## Record pumping

Track each side, record amounts and review the completed session. Overall elapsed time and side-specific measurements have different meanings and should not be conflated.

## Plan and record medicines

A catalogue can prefill a medicine's identity, strength and form. The caregiver enters the prescribed amount and routine. Daily clock times are distinct from an elapsed-hour interval. Recording a dose requires an explicit save; opening a notification does not mean medicine was given.

Historical records preserve the actual recorded dose and strength. Planned doses and reminder delivery are not proof of administration. This showcase supplies no treatment recommendations or dosing table.

## Review together

Shared caregiver access is governed by family membership and permissions. Product improvements focus on clear forms, sensible defaults, useful status language and fewer unnecessary steps.

All examples in this repository are fictional. No family profile, medical record or production screenshot is included.
